A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

so, who am i?

↙

I'm Exire. I like making things that feel right — not just things that technically work.

Most of my time goes into Roblox development: reanimation, combat ideas, effects, animation tools, and figuring out how to make an idea feel like its own thing. I can spend far too long on one small detail, but that's usually the bit that makes a project memorable.

This page is just my own little space to put the projects, links, and stuff I want people to find. Nothing too serious. Have a look around.
